And Wander

Stockholm, Sweden

Strategy, Photography

2026

Collaborators:

Set Design — Mary Smith Casting — Julian Reyes Production — Clara Thompson Art Direction — Leo Hammond

Category:

Commercial

Creative direction and photography for a seasonal narrative rooted in utility and restraint. Shot across a mix of constructed sets and natural environments, the project treated outdoor wear as an everyday system rather than specialist equipment. Emphasis was placed on texture, layering, and silhouette, allowing garments to be read through posture and environment rather than action.
