Otto Wotta

Water & beverages

Otto Wotta is an ecology-focused water and beverage company built around a more conscious relationship with natural resources. The brand explores the connection between hydration, environment, and everyday rituals through a contemporary approach to sustainable consumption.

The identity is shaped by the language of nature, flowing forms, organic structures, and a restrained visual rhythm inspired by water cycles and natural landscapes.

Soft typography, tactile materials, and thoughtful packaging create a system that feels pure yet expressive, balancing environmental responsibility with a modern, refined presence.
